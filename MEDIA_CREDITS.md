## Cinematic scene films

`destinations.mp4`, `culture.mp4`, `leadership.mp4` and `gallery.mp4` are original edits (slow
Ken Burns moves and cross-fades) assembled from the licensed photographs above; their poster frames
are cropped from the same photographs. `developers.mp4` is a generated abstract motion graphic
(emerald gradients, gold light sweeps, data grid) and contains no third-party footage. All films are
muted, H.264, faststart-encoded loops.

Developer portraits in `public/media/team/` are supplied by the developers themselves and are not
covered by the Wikimedia licences.
